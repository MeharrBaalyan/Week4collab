Whats in My Fridge?

CODING PROJECT!

Welcome to the most honest corner of my kitchen. This website is a digital peek inside my fridge; part food diary, part personality test, and part reminder of everything I forgot I bought. Every shelf tells a different story. Some ingredients are everyday essentials, some are leftovers waiting for a second chance, and others have been sitting there long enough to become part of the fridge itself.

About the Fridge

My fridge is never perfectly organized, but it reflects how I actually eat. It holds quick breakfasts, midnight snacks, unfinished meals, hopeful vegetables, and sauces that somehow survive every clean-out. Click on an item to discover why its there, how often I use it, and what I might make with it, if Im feeling ambitious.
